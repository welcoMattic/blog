July 2025, the Symfony AI was launched as an initiative, just like Symfony UX, to help developers integrate AI into their apps with ready-to-use components and bundles.

In this talk, let's discover Symfony AI's origins, explore its components, and learn how to use them with any AI platform. 

We'll also cover how to get started using it in your PHP apps and contribute to the project!
